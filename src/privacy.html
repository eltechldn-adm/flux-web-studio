Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

We are Flux Web Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website and use our services.

[OWNER INPUT REQUIRED]: Please provide your legal trading name, whether you are a sole trader or limited company (with registered number), your registered office/business address, and VAT registration status if applicable.

2. What information we collect

When you submit a project enquiry through our Project Brief form, we collect the following information:

  • Contact details: Your full name and email address.
  • Business details (optional): Your company name and website.
  • Project information: Details about your automation interests, current workflows, project urgency, and estimated budget (optional).

For operational and security purposes, our systems also process temporary metadata such as your IP address, browser type, and server requests when you visit our website.

3. How we collect your information

We collect your information directly from you when you voluntarily submit our Project Brief form or contact us via email. Operational security data is collected automatically by our infrastructure providers when you browse the site.

4. How and why we use your information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Review and respond to your project enquiries.
  • Evaluate if our services are a good fit for your business.
  • Communicate with you regarding potential or active projects.
  • Ensure the security and reliability of our website.

We do not use your information for unrelated marketing profiles or newsletters unless you explicitly opt in to such communications in the future.

5. Our lawful bases for processing

Under UK data protection law, we rely on the following lawful bases to process your information:

  • Legitimate interests: To respond to your business enquiries and evaluate potential projects.
  • Legal obligation & Legitimate interests: To maintain the security, integrity, and operational safety of our website.
  • Contract: To take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract or Statement of Work.

6. Service providers and processors

We use trusted third-party infrastructure to host our website, process forms, and route emails securely. This includes Cloudflare (Pages, Workers, and Email Routing). We only share the information strictly necessary for these services to function.

7. Cloudflare Turnstile

To protect our Project Brief form from spam and automated abuse, we use Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile performs essential security checks to verify that you are a human. During this process, it may securely process your IP address and device metadata. Turnstile does not use this data for targeted advertising or track you across other websites.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website is designed to respect your privacy. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. The only technologies we use are strictly necessary security tokens (such as those required by Cloudflare Turnstile) to protect our site from malicious activity. Because these are strictly necessary for security, a cookie consent banner is not required.

9. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers, such as Cloudflare, operate globally. This means your data (such as enquiry details and security metadata) may be processed outside the UK. We ensure our providers adhere to appropriate safeguards recognized by UK data protection laws, such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Data retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, manage any resulting business relationship, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. [OWNER INPUT REQUIRED: If you have a specific deletion timeframe for rejected leads, e.g., 6 months, insert it here.]

11. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including secure form processing, TLS encryption for data in transit, and isolated serverless environments (Cloudflare Workers).

12. Your privacy rights

Under UK law, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: You can request that we delete your data in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction or Objection: You can ask us to limit how we use your data or object to our processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.

13. Right to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). You can find out more on their website: ico.org.uk.

14. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended strictly for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

16. Contact us

If you have any questions or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at: hello@fluxwebstudio.com
