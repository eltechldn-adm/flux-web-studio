Solution Examples

We believe in setting clear expectations. Here are examples of the types of software architecture, AI tools, and interfaces we design and deploy.

CONCEPT EXAMPLE

Custom Operations CRM

The Problem

Off-the-shelf CRMs forced the team to use bloated interfaces with 50 fields they never used, causing staff to abandon the tool and use spreadsheets instead.

What Would Be Built

A focused, lightning-fast web application tailored exactly to their 5-stage sales cycle. Core functionality includes role-based access, instant search, and automated email logging.

Business Outcome

A system the team actually uses, providing management with accurate pipeline data without the monthly per-seat SaaS fees.

IN-HOUSE BUILD

AI Knowledge Assistant

The Problem

Onboarding new hires took weeks because crucial operating procedures were scattered across Google Drive, Notion, and Slack threads.

What Was Built

A secure, private RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) application. Employees ask questions in natural language, and the AI retrieves the exact internal document, summarizes the answer, and links to the source.

Business Outcome

Instant answers to procedural questions, allowing senior staff to focus on deep work instead of repeatedly answering "where is the policy for X?"
