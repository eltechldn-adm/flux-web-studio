Solution Examples

We believe in setting clear expectations. Here are examples of the types of problems Flux can solve and the software we could build around them.

CONCEPT EXAMPLE

Custom Operations CRM

The Problem

A sales team may outgrow an off-the-shelf CRM when unnecessary fields and rigid workflows push staff back toward spreadsheets.

What Would Be Built

Flux could build a focused CRM around a five-stage sales process. Core functionality may include role-based access, instant search, and automated email logging.

Potential Business Value

This could help centralise prospect data, reduce the risk of leads falling through the cracks, and cut down on administrative tracking.

CONCEPT EXAMPLE

AI Knowledge Assistant

The Problem

A growing team may struggle with onboarding when operating procedures are scattered across multiple tools, such as Google Drive, Notion, and Slack threads.

What Would Be Built

Flux could build a secure, private RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) application. Employees would ask questions in natural language, and the AI retrieves the exact internal document, summarises the answer, and links to the source.

Potential Business Value

Designed to make internal knowledge easier to find and reduce time spent searching through shared drives and manuals.
