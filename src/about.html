About Flux

We exist because businesses shouldn't need a corporate IT budget or a six-month timeline to get software that actually works the way they do.

FWS

Flux Web Studio is a UK-based, founder-led software studio. We communicate directly, speak plain English, and take responsibility for what we build.

Why small is an advantage

When you hire a massive agency, your project often gets passed down to junior developers while you speak to account managers. At Flux, you deal directly with the engineers scoping and building your software. Decisions are made in minutes, not weeks.

The AI Delivery Advantage

We embrace advanced AI engineering tools internally. We don't just sell "AI tools" to clients—we use AI to write boilerplate code, test faster, and deploy more reliably. This is why a small studio can deliver enterprise-grade custom applications quickly, passing the cost savings to you.

Who we help

We partner with founders, SMEs, operations teams, and consultants who know what they want their software to achieve, but don't want to get bogged down in the technical minutiae.
