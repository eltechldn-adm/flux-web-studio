Common Questions

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. Most clients come to us with a business problem ("I spend 10 hours a week doing X") rather than a technical specification. We will help map out the exact software requirements.

Can you work with my existing systems?

Yes. If your current tools have an API, we can usually build custom dashboards, integrations, or automation that connects to them seamlessly.

Who owns the finished software?

You receive the custom project deliverables agreed in your scope. Third-party services, frameworks and open-source components remain subject to their own licences.

Do you use AI to develop applications?

Yes, we use advanced AI-assisted engineering to write boilerplate code, test faster, and prototype rapidly. This allows us to deliver high-quality custom software in weeks instead of months, saving you money. A human engineer still reviews and tests all production code.

Got something you wish your software did?

Tell us what's slowing you down, what you've been trying to build, or what your current systems don't do properly. You don't need a technical specification — that's our job.
